## Yossi Shemer

Product and marketing · Be'er Sheva, open to Tel Aviv

I work where audience research meets product decisions: finding out who a product is for, deciding what to build and what to leave out, and writing the message that explains it. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the Open University of Israel (2026), and I use AI coding agents to turn specs into working prototypes early, so decisions are tested on real users rather than argued in documents.

Since 2019 I have worked at the Yachdav Association, supporting adults with special needs and coordinating the instructors' shifts. Since October 2026 I also sit on its AI steering committee. I am currently a participant in From Prompt to Production at the Google and Reichman AI Tech School, a 12-week practicum run with Wix and Base44, where each team ships one product on Base44 for a real client.

### Selected work

- **Out for Career**, a members-only community site for alumni of a career-change program. A needs survey of 19 alumni ranked lectures first and mentoring last, so the planned mentoring bot was dropped in favor of events and a member directory. I wrote the spec, set the visual identity, produced the teaser and launch film, and built the site. It is live and in iterations with the community leaders before opening to all members.
- **Shift scheduling for a 24/7 care facility.** The spec came from recorded interviews with the coordinators, who are satisfied with their current paid tool, so the bar was set at fewer manual corrections after each automatic run. The pilot's pass/fail criteria were written before any code. In development.
- **Audience messaging for a business-consulting firm.** One investment model, three audiences, a message for each, and short videos to carry them, all reviewed against a claims list before the client's sign-off.
- **Research tooling for Yad L'Neshima (CCHS Israel)**, as a volunteer. A tagging scheme for the research literature on a rare breathing disorder, developed with the chief scientist and piloted on 14 papers over eight feedback rounds. The model proposes, an expert confirms.
- **Landing page for a men's circle facilitator.** Positioning, copy and build, specified together with the founder.
- **AI workshops since 2024**, for non-technical and developer audiences, with an open starter kit.

The client and community projects hold personal data, so their repositories are private. The public repositories here are small open-source tools that came out of that work. I am glad to walk through specs, screens and decisions in conversation.

### How I work

I write the spec and the acceptance criteria, agents do most of the building, and I verify what ships. For AI features, the model reads the unstructured input and plain code makes the decision, so the same input always gives the same result.
